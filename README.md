# Electrical Appliances and Lamps / 电器与灯具

> A Minecraft mod that adds various electrical appliances and lamps to make your house more realistic.
>
> 一个为 Minecraft 添加各种电器和灯具、让房屋更逼真的模组。

## Important / 重要说明

- **`assets/`** is the **active folder** — this is where I accept contributions. If you want to help, focus on **translations, models, or textures**.
  
- **Other files** (including source code) are **passive/reference-only** — they exist for transparency but are not part of the active development workflow.

---

- **`assets/`** 是 **活跃文件夹** —— 这是我接受贡献的地方。如果你想帮忙，请专注于**翻译、模型或纹理**。
  
- **其他文件**（包括源代码）是 **被动/仅供参考的** —— 它们存在是为了透明，但不属于活跃开发流程的一部分。
